# Content formats and viral patterns in the stick-figure / stickman faceless YouTube niche (state as of Nov 2026)

> **Method note (read first).** This session's network blocked direct page fetches (youtube.com, outlierkit.com, saturaai.com, blog.autonolab.com, facelesslist.com, invideo.io all failed to resolve). Everything below comes from **web-search result summaries/snippets** of those pages, not from reading the full pages. Consequences: (1) view counts are third-party tracker estimates (OutlierKit, vidIQ, youtubers.me), not read off YouTube; (2) publish dates for individual videos were almost never available; (3) no Reddit (r/NewTubers, r/PartneredYoutube) or X thread content could be retrieved; (4) most qualitative claims come from tool-vendor/SEO blogs with an incentive to promote the niche. Items I could not source are in the Gaps sections, not asserted as facts.

## 1. What are the recurring video formats, and which get the most views?

### Takeaway
The formats with verified large view counts are (a) "Every X Explained in N Minutes" list-explainers, (b) morbid-curiosity / dark-history / "what dying would be like" lists in MS-Paint-stickman style, (c) deadpan comedy explainers (Casually Explained), (d) comedic history told with simple figures (OverSimplified), and (e) survival / "what-if" stickman scenarios. The single biggest verified outliers skew toward morbid curiosity and relatable social topics rather than pure "tutorial" content.

### Cited Findings
- **"Every [X] Explained in [Y] Minutes" is a highly templated faceless format.** OutlierKit describes Stickman 616 as a "highly templated, faceless 'Every [X] Explained in [Y] Minutes' format" (note: this channel is Marvel lore, not actually stick-figure animation). — [OutlierKit: Stickman 616](https://outlierkit.com/channel/stickman616x)
- **The Analyst** (602.0K subs, 70.4M total views, 277 uploads, ~2.5 years old, ~100% long-form, avg ~15:25, ~3.5 uploads/week): most-viewed video "Every Government Form Explained in 12 Minutes" at 4.4M views (~71x the channel's typical video); biggest recent breakout "Every Hidden Advantage of Your Skin Colour" at 747.0K. Page snapshot was ~82 days old at search time (roughly Aug 2026). — [OutlierKit: The Analyst](https://outlierkit.com/channel/theanalystyts)
- **The Paint Explainer** (about 2.02M subs, about 326.8M total views, 170 videos as of 14 Nov 2026 per vidIQ): OutlierKit categorises it as "Morbid Curiosity & Dark History Infographics"; most-viewed video "What Dying on Every Planet Would Be Like" at 12.0M views (~12.2x typical); biggest recent breakout "The Creepiest Internet Mysteries That Were Finally Solved" at 6.2M; recent uploads average ~13:39. — [OutlierKit: The Paint Explainer](https://outlierkit.com/channel/thepaintexplainer); [vidIQ stats](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@thepaintexplainer/)

- **Sleep / "boring history" is a real adjacent format but not stick-figure**: 404 Media (Sept 2025) reported AI-generated "Boring History for Sleep" videos flooding YouTube; e.g. "Boring History for Sleep | How Medieval PEASANTS Survived the Coldest Nights" by Sleepless Historian at ~2.3M views, with the AI voice glitching ~1h15m in. These use AI imagery, not stick figures. — [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/ai-generated-boring-history-videos-are-flooding-youtube-and-drowning-out-real-history/)
- **Reference catalogue**: FacelessList maintains a "15 Faceless stick figure animation channels" category (defined as relying on "simple stick figure drawings to communicate stories and concepts"). — [FacelessList](https://facelesslist.com/categories/stick-figure-animations/)

### Inferences
- By verified peak views (excluding the legacy giants), ranking is roughly: morbid-curiosity lists (12.0M, 6.2M, 5.8M) > "Every X Explained" bureaucracy/psychology lists (4.4M, 3.7M) > survival/what-if (3.3M) > biology/anthropology curiosities (940K). Sample is small and tracker-based.
- The extreme outlier multipliers on small channels (138x, 71x) suggest the format is hit-driven: one topic/packaging match carries a channel, rather than steady per-video performance.
- "Stick figure" covers two visual sub-styles in practice: clean vector stickmen (Productive Peter / easy, actually style) and crude MS-Paint drawings (Paint Explainer / Simple Paint / Explain In Paint).

### Gaps
- "Your life as a ...", "Why you'd never survive ...", iceberg/tier explainers, money-lesson and psychology-facts stick-figure videos: I found **no** specific stick-figure videos with view counts for these exact formats. They may exist; searches returned nothing verifiable.
- Whether The Analyst's visuals are strictly stick-figure could not be visually confirmed (pages not fetchable).
- No ViewStats data retrieved.

## 3. What script structure, pacing, tone, narration style, length?

### Takeaway
Verified data covers length (recent-upload averages of ~10.5 to ~18 minutes across tracked channels) and tone descriptors (deadpan, dry humour, parody-educational). I found almost no sourced data on script structure, word counts, or AI-vs-human voice share.

### Cited Findings
- Average recent video length by channel: Stickman 616 ~10:33; Beanie Survives ~12:24; The Paint Explainer ~13:39; The Analyst ~15:25; Mr Sticky ~17:54. — [OutlierKit: Stickman 616](https://outlierkit.com/channel/stickman616x); [Beanie Survives](https://outlierkit.com/channel/beaniesurvives); [Paint Explainer](https://outlierkit.com/channel/thepaintexplainer); [The Analyst](https://outlierkit.com/channel/theanalystyts); [Mr Sticky](https://outlierkit.com/channel/telopintofacil)
- Upload cadence varies widely: The Analyst ~3.5/week; Explain In Paint ~6.9/week; Stick Alive and MR SAGE state weekly. — [OutlierKit: The Analyst](https://outlierkit.com/channel/theanalystyts); [OutlierKit: Explain In Paint](https://outlierkit.com/channel/explaininpaint1); [YouTube: Stick Alive](https://www.youtube.com/@1StickAlive)
- Tone: one 2026 roundup describes a mid-tier stick-figure channel as "whiteboard-style animation and deadpan narration" with 500M+ total views from under 100 videos; Casually Explained is described as "comedy explainers with stick-figure animation and dry humor". — [Autonolab comedy niche post](https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2025-11-09-faceless-youtube-comedy/); [Autonolab animation niche post](https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2026-01-01-faceless-youtube-animation/)
- Second-person framing is used by survival channels: Stick Alive asks whether the viewer would survive each scenario; Beanie Survives uses day-by-day scenario structure. — [YouTube: Stick Alive](https://www.youtube.com/@1StickAlive); [OutlierKit: Beanie Survives](https://outlierkit.com/channel/beaniesurvives)
- Production stack in 2026 tutorials: ChatGPT for scripts and ElevenLabs for voiceover, with AI image/animation tools; one tutorial notes that a 10-minute video implies a large script and many scenes to manage. — [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HPE1NJuoPWo); [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zpoXJrb5L3Y)
- The key script warning from a 2026 analysis: "basic visuals won't save a video with a weak idea"; weak scripts are the bigger risk than weak visuals. — [Satura AI blog](https://saturaai.com/blog/why-stick-figure-youtube-videos-keep-breaking-out-in-2026-jpkuyd)

### Inferences
- At a typical narration pace of roughly 150 words/minute (my assumption, not sourced), 10-18 minute videos imply roughly 1,500-2,700 words. Treat as an estimate only.
- "Every X" list videos are structurally modular (N short self-contained segments), which gives natural re-hooks every 30-90 seconds; survival videos use chronological escalation (Day 1, Day 2...). Inferred from titles/descriptions.
- AI voiceover appears common among new entrants (tutorials all assume ElevenLabs), while the legacy leaders (Casually Explained, OverSimplified) are known for human creator voices; I have no measured split.

## 4. How do long-form and Shorts perform relative to each other?

### Takeaway
The tracked successful explainer channels are essentially 100% long-form (10-18 min). Shorts can spike (one 4.2M Short) but decay quickly across a series and monetize poorly; no head-to-head niche dataset was found.

### Cited Findings
- The Analyst and Beanie Survives each publish about 100% long-form among recent uploads. — [OutlierKit: The Analyst](https://outlierkit.com/channel/theanalystyts); [OutlierKit: Beanie Survives](https://outlierkit.com/channel/beaniesurvives)
- Stickocalypse AI stick-figure Shorts series: Part 1 reached 4.2M views, later parts 270K, 142K, 60K; channel at 35,600 subs. The author suggests the algorithm pushes Part 1 to new viewers while later parts rely on returning viewers. — [Vuela.ai](https://vuela.ai/blog/how-to-make-viral-stick-figure-series-with-ai)
- A reported stickman channel with 42 videos, 217,000 subs and 15M+ views works out to ~357K views/upload. — [Income Reality Check](https://income-reality-check.pages.dev/a/how-to-create-stickman-animation-with-ai-100-free/)
- Critical view: a channel showing 2.3M views from 10 videos is a top outlier; "for every channel pulling millions, there are thousands of near-identical stickman uploads that the feed never surfaces"; Shorts RPMs are "pennies per thousand views"; templated AI output risks YouTube's inauthentic-content policy. — [Income Reality Check](https://income-reality-check.pages.dev/a/how-to-create-stickman-animation-with-ai-100-free/)
- A separate 2026 blog questions whether "funny stickman AI shorts" is a real automation niche. — [Satura AI](https://saturaai.com/blog/funny-stickman-ai-shorts-is-this-real-youtube-automation-niche-qzatpq)
- Unverified RPM claim for the stick-figure niche: roughly $5-$17 (AI-generated note, no sourcing). — [Lilys.ai note](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/openai-agent-builder-20260208/best-faceless-niches-2026-no-competition)

### Inferences
- Long-form is where the durable channels sit; Shorts work as discovery for serialized fiction/comedy but show steep part-to-part drop-off (4.2M to 60K, a ~98.6% decline by part 4).
- For an explainer channel, Shorts are best treated as a top-of-funnel test, not the core product.

### Gaps
- No dataset comparing Shorts vs long-form views/RPM specifically for stick-figure channels. No verified RPM figures.

## 5. Why does the simple stick-figure style work?

### Takeaway
Sources attribute it to lower viewer resistance/accessibility, very low production cost enabling high cadence, and attention shifting to the script and idea; the main counter-argument is survivorship bias and that the style does not rescue weak ideas.

### Cited Findings
- "The simple style lowers viewer resistance" (cited in 2026 niche guides with Productive Peter and Easy Actually as examples). — [Lilys.ai note](https://lilys.ai/en/notes/openai-agent-builder-20260208/best-faceless-niches-2026-no-competition)
- "Simple stickman animations make complex biological and anthropological concepts highly accessible and engaging." — [OutlierKit: CurioStick](https://outlierkit.com/channel/curiostickyt)
- The format "can outperform polished edits", but should be tested "as a production model, not as a guaranteed niche shortcut"; weak scripts are the larger risk. — [Satura AI](https://saturaai.com/blog/why-stick-figure-youtube-videos-keep-breaking-out-in-2026-jpkuyd)
- Low production cost enables extreme cadence: Explain In Paint ~6.9 videos/week; The Analyst ~3.5/week. — [OutlierKit: Explain In Paint](https://outlierkit.com/channel/explaininpaint1); [OutlierKit: The Analyst](https://outlierkit.com/channel/theanalystyts)
- AI tooling makes the style producible "without filming, drawing, or editing" per 2026 tutorials (creator-promotional). — [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YoDCFb65gJ0); [YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dpfTXbMQAJ8)
- Long track record: Alan Becker's stick-figure animations have 9.9B+ total views; OverSimplified has run since 2016. So the 2025-26 wave is a revival of an established style. — [Wikipedia: Stick figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stick_figure); [Podcastle](https://podcastle.ai/blog/who-is-oversimplified)
- Saturation: an audit of 43 faceless animation channels rated market saturation "Medium", saturated with creators but demand still high. — [Autonolab](https://blog.autonolab.com/niches/2026-01-01-faceless-youtube-animation/)
- Policy risk: commentary in 2025 on YouTube's policy against mass-produced/inauthentic AI content discusses faceless channels, citing Productive Peter as an example of a distinctive cartoon identity with consistent tone. — [InVideo blog](https://invideo.io/blog/youtube-kills-ai-faceless-channels/)

### Inferences
- A stick figure is a blank avatar: viewers can project themselves onto it, which fits second-person survival and self-improvement framing. (My inference; no psychology source found.)
- Because the look is commoditized and AI-reproducible, defensibility comes from topic selection, script voice, and a recognizable character/thumbnail identity.

### Gaps
- No academic or analyst source on viewer psychology specific to stick figures was found. No Reddit/X practitioner testimony could be retrieved.
